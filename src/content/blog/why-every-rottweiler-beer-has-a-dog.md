A Rottweiler is calm until it matters. It knows its people, holds its ground and never cuts corners. We wanted beers that behave the same way. Then we noticed something. Each of our four beers already had a character of its own.

## The Guardian

Our Strong Lager is the big dog. It is 7.5% and full bodied, yet smooth, with no harsh alcohol bite. It rests at least 30 days in a cold cellar before it leaves. Steady, patient and reliable, like a guard dog lying at the door.

## The Runner

Our Dry Lager is lean and quick. It is light and crisp, with a bitterness that snaps off fast. It also takes the longest rest of the four, at least 36 days in the cellar, which is what makes it so clean.

## The Bite

Our IPA has attitude. Citrus, tropical fruit and resin come first, then a firm bitterness that lingers. It is never pasteurised and stays cold from our tank to your tap, so the hops taste the way we brewed them.

## The Sunbather

Our Witbier is the easy one. Hazy, soft and gently tart, with coriander and orange peel. It is the dog lying in a patch of afternoon sun, in no hurry at all.
