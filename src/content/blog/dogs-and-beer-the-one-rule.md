We are a brewery named after a dog. So the most important thing we will ever write about dogs is short.

## Never give your dog beer

Not a sip, not a lick from the glass, not a splash in the water bowl. Alcohol is far more dangerous for a dog than for a person, even in small amounts. Hops are a problem too. In dogs they can cause a dangerous rise in body temperature.

If your dog does drink beer or eat hops, call your vet straight away. Do not wait to see what happens.

## Bringing your dog to the pub

A good pub dog is a calm dog, which is very Rottweiler of them. A few habits keep everyone happy:

- Put glasses on the table, never on the floor or a low bench.
- Bring water and a bowl, especially on a hot day.
- Find a cool spot out of the way of feet and trays.
- Keep snacks with onion, garlic or chocolate away from curious noses.
- Head home when your dog has had enough of the crowd.

## Why we care

The Rottweiler is loyal, calm and protective. Those are the values we brew by. Looking after the dogs in our lives is part of that.

Raise a glass. Give your dog a biscuit.
